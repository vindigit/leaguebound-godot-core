Behavior source is frozen commit `8b8fd437ca700beef14886b61f95f7ce9eb77c93`.
Godot is `4.7.1.stable.official.a13da4feb`. This evidence concerns fixture
construction only; it does not certify game outcomes or §27.1.

`construction_audit.json` was generated by
`Godot --headless --path . -s tools/audit_population_pairing.gd`.
`construction_audit_metadata.json` records source hashes and command provenance.
The archive contains all 117 existing rounded 10-player × 20-rating roster
vectors, with actual offensive/defensive capability indices, per competition.
"Legacy" means the old adjacent pairing applied to these unchanged rosters,
not a run of an old engine.

The old raw ladder difference has two values: +2 steps in 11/13 cases and −11
steps in 2/13, or +0.70 and −3.85 rating-level points. This describes the raw
team tilt, not each rounded player rating or complete capability gap. Across
234 old fixtures (the full roster period crossed with opening parity), actual
capability gaps have 198 positive and 36 negative values, zero ties, and mean
zero in every competition. Thus equal average strength alone did not prove
venue exchangeability.

The corrected full 54,756-fixture joint cycle has 27,144 positive and 27,144
negative gaps, 468 ties, and mean zero in every competition. Root-mean-square
capability gaps change as follows; no target or tolerance was used to choose

Every aligned quartet crosses its actual two rosters over both venue and
opening status. An aligned 468-fixture block has uniform full-roster marginals
but only 231 ordered roster cells and 25 of 169 ladder-pair cells after both orientations. The 117 base orientations alone cover 117 roster cells and 13 ladder cells. It is not
an independent local joint population. Partial quartets need not balance;
explicit venue offsets and fixed-opener diagnostic arms intentionally override
these symmetries. The construction proof makes no home-win, overtime, scoring,
or pace claim.
