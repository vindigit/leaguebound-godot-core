are construction facts. An aligned 468-fixture block has 117 ordered roster
cells and 13 ladder cells in its base orientation, and 231 ordered roster cells
and 25 ladder cells after including reversals. Full Cartesian enumeration takes
54,756 fixtures. Local balance does not imply independent random joint draws,
and the actual rounded roster/capability distributions must not be equated with
the raw two-point ladder difference.

All twenty diagnosis cells are complete: 9,360 games. Independent review accepts
their integrity and arithmetic, not overall calibration success. Failure
inventories remain in `diagnosis_independent_review.json`. Candidate home-arm
possession and PPP verdicts pass on this diagnostic range; that is not replicated
closure of every band. Both holdouts remain required.

Nonsignificant home-effect contrasts cannot exclude a population interaction.
Moreover, a blanket statement that all diagnostic interactions are
nonsignificant would itself be false: the high-school paired home-win
interaction interval excludes zero in the exploratory diagnosis. This selected
single-range result is not a replicated finding. A population home-win row is
not the controlled equal-team home-court estimand, and two arms cannot establish
the locked three-arm venue verdict. Mirrored overtime results do not exclude
every roster contribution to the shortfall.

The three college snapshot-pin corrections are disclosed with the original
three failures, matched observations and 22/22 revised pass. The original
24-game sample, seeds and tolerances remain fixed. These fixture snapshots do
not prove unchanged population rates. No production engine, rule profile,
shooting, pace, target or tolerance change is justified by this review; the
candidate construction change and narrow int64 consumer preservation are
identified separately from those unchanged contracts.

No equivalence, unique overtime cause, Stage 4 completion, downstream tuning
authorization or section 27.1 certification follows from the current evidence.
Final status and PR wording require another review after the outstanding
